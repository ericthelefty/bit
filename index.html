    How to write them

    One prompt per line. Add performers after a bar: A sea shanty about a parking ticket | Dana & Luis

    Start a section with ## For Two Performers. Its name shows under each prompt in that section.

    Video prompts: start the line with video:, then the YouTube link, then an optional time range and caption:

    video: https://youtu.be/abc123XYZ90 0:30-1:45 Dub this nature doc | Dana & Luis

    The screen shows the prompt card first. Press Next to play the clip full screen, then Next again to move on. The time range is optional, and a ?t= in the link also sets the start.

    Minigames: start a section with Minigame: It’s D’improv (Take Some Direction). The part in parentheses shows as a subtitle on the minigame card.

    Directions: indent bullets under a line and each one becomes its own step. The line stays on screen, small, and each Next reveals the next direction.

    * “That’s what she said.”
       * Italian.
       * More Italian.

    Bullets are stripped, blank lines are skipped, and lines starting with a single # are notes that never show.

    How it works

    ## Performers makes a heading. A plain line is a name. Sound | Dana puts a role and a name side by side. A blank line adds extra space. Lines starting with a single # are notes that never show.

    The credits come after the end card. Press C or Roll credits on the Run show tab to start them from anywhere. They scroll up to the logo, hold it for a few seconds, then start again from the top until you leave them. While they roll, P pauses and R starts them over.

    Starts when the credits start and fades out when you leave them. Pick one of your sounds from the Sounds tab (turn on Loop there if it's shorter than the credits).

    Drop audio files here MP3, WAV, M4A, AAC, OGG or FLAC

    Sounds are saved in this browser on this Mac, so you only add them once. If you switch browsers or computers, add them again there. The first 10 sounds get keys 1–0 and the next 10 get ⇧+1–0. Use the arrows to reorder. Loop sounds (like walk-in music) repeat until you press their key again, then fade out. Other sounds restart each time you press them.

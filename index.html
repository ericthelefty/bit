    Performers

    Assign performers to any prompt, minigame line or direction with the Assign button in the running order on Run show. Their names show on the projector and are written into the script after a |.

    How to write them

    One prompt per line. Add performers after a bar: A sea shanty about a parking ticket | Dana & Luis

    Start a section with ## For Two Performers. Its name shows under each prompt in that section.

    Video prompts: start the line with video:, then the YouTube link, then an optional time range and caption:

    video: https://youtu.be/abc123XYZ90 0:30-1:45 Dub this nature doc | Dana & Luis

    Add vol:60 (or 60%) after the link to set that clip's volume, e.g. video: https://youtu.be/abc123XYZ90 vol:40 | Aaron. Moving the console's video volume slider during the clip overrides it.

    The screen shows the prompt card first. Press Next to play the clip full screen, then Next again to move on. The time range is optional, and a ?t= in the link also sets the start.

    Minigames: start a section with Minigame: It’s D’improv (Take Some Direction). The part in parentheses shows as a subtitle on the minigame card.

    Directions: indent bullets under a line and each one becomes its own step. The line stays on screen, small, and each Next reveals the next direction. Add | Name after a direction to show whose turn it is.

    * “That’s what she said.”
       * Italian.
       * More Italian.

    Sound for a card: add [sfx: Air horn] anywhere on a line (a prompt, a direction, a video line or a section heading) and that sound plays when the card comes up. Use the sound's name from the Sounds tab, or its pad number like [sfx: 3]. No other sounds play on their own; only tagged cards (and the sound board when you press it).

    Bullets are stripped, blank lines are skipped, and lines starting with a single # are notes that never show.

    How it works

    ## Performers makes a heading. A plain line is a name. Sound | Dana puts a role and a name side by side. A blank line adds extra space. Lines starting with a single # are notes that never show.

    The credits come after the end card. Press C or Roll credits on the Run show tab to start them from anywhere. They scroll all the way off the top of the screen, and after a moment of black they start again from the top until you leave them. While they roll, P pauses and R starts them over.

    Music: add [sfx: Song name] on any line of the credits to play that sound while they roll. It starts with the credits and fades out when you leave them. Turn on Loop for it on the Sounds tab if it's shorter than the credits. With no tag, the credits roll silently.

    Drop audio files here MP3, WAV, M4A, AAC, OGG or FLAC

    Sounds are saved in this browser on this Mac, so you only add them once. If you switch browsers or computers, add them again there. The first 10 sounds get keys 1–0 and the next 10 get ⇧+1–0. Use the arrows to reorder. Loop sounds (like walk-in music) repeat until you stop them. Pressing a sound's key or pad while it's playing stops it, and starting any sound stops whatever else is playing.
